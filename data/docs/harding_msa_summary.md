# MSA Summary — Blaugarnet Services Inc. ↔ Harding Outfitters
**Prepared by:** Blaugarnet Services legal ops, for delivery team reference · **MSA executed:** 2026-08-10
*This is a plain-language summary. The executed MSA governs.*

- **Term:** 3 years from effective date, auto-renewing annually.
- **Payment terms:** invoices due **net 45** from receipt. Disputed amounts must be flagged within 10 business days.
- **Invoicing:** milestone-based per SOW; expenses billed at cost with prior written approval.
- **IP:** work product is Harding's on payment; Blaugarnet Services retains pre-existing tools and generalized know-how.
- **Confidentiality:** mutual, 5 years; customer personal data handled under the executed **Data Processing Addendum (Exhibit C)**.
- **Liability cap:** 12 months of fees under the applicable SOW; carve-outs for confidentiality and data breach.
- **Compliance:** Blaugarnet Services to maintain SOC 2 Type II; Harding security questionnaire and pen-test rights per Exhibit D.
- **Termination:** either party, 60 days notice for convenience; Harding pays for work performed to date.
- **Change control:** changes to any SOW require a written change order executed by both parties. The MSA is silent on approval bodies; the SOW defines the operating process.
