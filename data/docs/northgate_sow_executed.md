# Statement of Work — Northgate Logistics: WMS Integration Layer
**Blaugarnet Services Inc. · Executed 2026-05-02 · SOW-2026-011**

## 1. Overview & Objectives
Blaugarnet Services will design and deliver an integration layer between Northgate Logistics' warehouse management system (StackWare WMS) and its order management platform, replacing nightly batch files with event-driven synchronization across 6 distribution centers.

## 2. Scope
In: event bus design and build; StackWare adapter; order/inventory sync services; monitoring dashboards; cutover per DC. Out: WMS configuration changes; carrier integrations; historical data re-processing.

## 3. Deliverables & Milestones
M1 integration design signed off; M2 adapter live in staging; M3 first DC cutover; M4 all 6 DCs live.

## 4. Timeline
Kickoff 2026-05-19 · staged cutovers Aug–Sep · **all DCs live by 2026-10-15**.

## 6. Governance

## 7. Client Responsibilities
