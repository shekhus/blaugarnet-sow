# Blaugarnet Services Inc. — Leave Policy (extract)
**HR handbook §5 · Updated 2026-01**

Annual leave: 24 days, accrued monthly, max carryover 6 days into Q1. Sick leave: 10 days, doctor's note beyond 3 consecutive days. Parental leave: 18 weeks primary / 6 weeks secondary, fully paid. Public holidays follow the office-location calendar. On client-site weeks, travel days are workdays, not leave. Leave during a committed UAT or go-live window requires delivery-lead approval — plan around your project calendar.
