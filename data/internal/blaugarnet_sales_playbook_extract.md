# Sales playbook — extract (§4 Commercial guardrails)
**Version 2026-04 · Owner: Meera Shah**

1. Milestone pricing over T&M wherever the client allows; never fixed-price beyond a discovery sprint without an approved risk memo.
2. Rates come off the current-year card. Any exception (legacy rates, discounts) requires a finance-approved deal note referenced in the SOW — a verbal commitment to a client MUST be converted to a deal note within 5 business days.
3. We do not participate in reverse auctions.
4. Payment terms default net 30 in proposals; the executed MSA always wins — check it before the SOW is issued.
5. Every SOW names the client-side acceptance authority. Post-Helios, no exceptions.
