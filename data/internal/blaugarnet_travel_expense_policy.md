# Blaugarnet Services Inc. — Travel & Expense Policy (extract)
**Finance handbook §3 · Updated 2025-09**

Client-billable travel requires prior written client approval per the MSA — no approval, no booking. Economy under 6 hours; premium economy beyond. Hotel caps by city tier (see appendix). Per diem replaces meal receipts for trips over 2 nights. Expenses filed within 30 days; billable expenses invoiced at cost with the client approval attached. Mileage at the standard IRS rate.
